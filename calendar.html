Each year has 13 months of 28 days (exactly four weeks), so every month in a year starts on the same weekday. The year begins on the day of the spring equinox, and the one or two Threshold Days left over close the year before the next equinox. The small moon on each day shows its phase at local noon; outlined days hold a new, first-quarter, full or last-quarter moon (hover for the exact time).
